## What the count means

These are distinct technical documents, not 473 peer-reviewed studies or independent organizations. The technical corpus includes 170 papers, 199 repository technical documents, 18 model cards, 67 official documentation pages, 16 source-code documents and three issue/implementation discussions.

Inspection depth is recorded per source: 445 selected substantive sections, 21 complete paper abstracts, four full short technical documents and three browser-inspected issue/PR discussions. Search snippets, failed fetches and identified duplicate documents were excluded. Selected browser checks supplemented direct HTTP retrieval; this was not 473 separate browser visits.

All three research reports passed the requested skill's 7/7-field validator. All 475 source records, including the two social posts, have a matching saved-content SHA256 hash in the working research archive. Hashes establish which text was captured; they do not independently validate author claims.

The rankings are editorial judgments. Hardware capacity, speedups, four-hour sample counts and audience response remain unmeasured. No proposed experiment was executed as part of this research report.
